'A neurally plausible implementation of predictive coding (PC) on a robot'

Extensive performance comparison with traditional state estimation technique Kalman filtering (KF). PC vastly outperforms KF in terms of speed, with only a minor impact on accuracy. This is a new finding that demonstrates PC as a promising approach for further robotics and biological investigation.
